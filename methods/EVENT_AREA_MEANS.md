# Event-area means

The area second pass uses the exact same original combo raster, previous/current ID rasters, common grid, biome mask and accepted-overlap threshold used by the verified territorial transition stage.

The previously published territorial event counts remain authoritative. Every new area task is rejected unless its birth, death, simple split and simple merge counts exactly reproduce the corresponding verified territorial counts for every biome/state/city row. Total previous/current area is also reconciled, and full-biome birth/death area is checked against the verified spatial metrics when available.

The stage is intentionally separate so the 468 existing territorial receipts and their provenance remain immutable.
